01 For league managers

Run the season.
Skip the scramble.

Give every part of your league a place. Keep teams, registrations, and games organized as your season gets going.

  • Make room for every division

    Set season dates, add divisions, and choose a registration fee for each competition.

  • Keep registrations in order

    Review team applications, track approvals and paid or unpaid fees, and export registration records.

  • Put the schedule in place

    Add games individually or import a CSV. Set times and venues, assign referees, and update details as plans change.

  • Keep results moving

    You or the assigned referee can record scores. Results feed directly into each division's standings.

02 For team managers

Your team.
Ready for what's next.

Build a team that lasts beyond one season. Keep your roster, competition registrations, and game schedule together.

  • Bring your roster together

    Create your team, choose its colors, and add players with existing accounts. Players can also join your team themselves.

  • Find your next competition

    Browse leagues and seasonal divisions, apply with your team, and follow your registration status across seasons.

  • Stay on top of game day

    See your team's games in one place. When SMS is enabled, team managers can receive text updates when game details or results change.

03 For players

Know when to play.
See where you stand.

The details you need, before and after the whistle. Sign in to find your team, follow the schedule, and catch up on results.

  • Find your people

    Join a team and see who's on the roster. Your team page brings your teammates and games together.

  • Know your next match

    Check the opponent, date, time, and venue so you know where to be and when.

  • Follow the season's story

    See match results and division standings, including wins, draws, goals, and points. Soccer scoring keeps the table up to date as results come in.
