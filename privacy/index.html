Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Agones LLC (“Agones,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, retains, and shares personal information when you use the Agones platform at agones.fit (the “Platform”). This Policy is incorporated into, and should be read together with, our Terms of Service.

1.Information We Collect

When you register and use the Platform, you provide personal information including:

  • Name and email address
  • Phone number (if you choose to enable SMS notifications)
  • Date of birth
  • Mailing address

This information is used to administer your account, facilitate your participation in league activity, and send you notifications relevant to your role.

2.Role-Based Data Access

Access to your personal information within the Platform is limited by your assigned user role:

  • League managers and administrators may view contact information for users within their league.
  • Team managers may view contact information for players on their team.
  • Players and referees have limited access to other users’ personal data, consistent with normal league operations.

3.Notifications and SMS

If you provide a phone number and opt in, Agones sends notifications related to your league activity, including:

  • Schedule updates and game reminders
  • Registration confirmations
  • Roster and attendance updates
  • Score and standings notifications

Notifications may be delivered by email and/or SMS text message. Message frequency varies based on your league’s activity and schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

How you opt in: you create an account at agones.fit, then in your profile’s notification settings you enter your phone number and check a box agreeing to receive SMS notifications from Agones LLC regarding your league activity. This checkbox is not pre-selected.

How you opt out: reply STOP to any SMS message at any time, or update your notification preferences in your account settings.

Your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in status are not shared with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes.

4.Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. If you request to delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 90 days, except where retention is required for legal, tax, or dispute-resolution purposes, including payment-related records, which we retain for 7 years.

5.Third-Party Services

Payment Processing

League registration fees and other payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe’s handling of your information is governed by the Stripe Services Agreement and Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Agones does not store, process, or have access to your credit card or banking information.

SMS and Communications

Agones uses a third-party messaging provider to deliver SMS and email notifications. This provider processes your phone number and message content solely to deliver notifications on Agones’s behalf and does not use this information for its own marketing purposes.

6.Data We Do Not Sell

Agones does not sell your personal information to third parties.

7.Children’s Privacy

The Platform is intended for use by adults aged 18 and older. Agones does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8.Your Choices

  • You may update or correct your personal information at any time in your account settings.
  • You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP or updating your notification preferences.
  • You may request deletion of your account at any time; see Section 4 (Data Retention) for what happens to your data afterward.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this document and, where appropriate, notify you via email or in-app notification. Your continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
